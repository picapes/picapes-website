## Introduction

PiCapes respects your privacy and aims to collect as little information as possible. This Privacy Policy explains what information PiCapes stores and how it is used.

By using PiCapes services, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Store

PiCapes may store information necessary to provide its services, including:

* Minecraft usernames (IGNs)
* Cape selections and cosmetic preferences
* Account settings and preferences
* Content voluntarily submitted to PiCapes, such as cape submissions, feedback, or support requests

PiCapes does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information.

## Optional Community Information

Some PiCapes community platforms, such as Discord servers, may offer optional roles, surveys, or profile customization features.

Information such as your country, region, language, interests, or similar details may be voluntarily provided by you. Participation in these features is entirely optional.

## How Information Is Used

Information stored by PiCapes is used solely to:

* Provide cape functionality and related services
* Manage user accounts and preferences
* Process cape submissions
* Improve the service and user experience
* Moderate the platform and prevent abuse

## Advertising and Third-Party Services

PiCapes may use third-party services, including advertising providers, hosting providers, analytics providers, content delivery networks, or authentication services.

These third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies and practices. PiCapes does not control how third-party services collect, store, or process information.

Users should review the privacy policies of any third-party services they interact with.

## Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate PiCapes services or maintain account functionality.

PiCapes may remove inactive accounts, unused data, or submitted content at its discretion.

## Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

You may request the removal of your PiCapes account and associated account data where technically feasible and legally permitted.

Certain information may be retained where required for operational, security, or legal purposes.

## Changes to This Policy

PiCapes may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Continued use of PiCapes after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

## Contact
