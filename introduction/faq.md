# Frequently Asked Questions
The following are some common questions that players have about PiCapes.

## What is PiCapes?
PiCapes is a Minecraft cape service that allows players to display their custom capes in various Minecraft clients.

## Is PiCapes free to use?
Yes! PiCapes is completely free to use for all Minecraft players. We even support offline/cracked accounts!

## How to use PiCapes?
You can use PiCapes by following the steps starting from the [Getting Started](/getting-started/installation.md) guide.

## Can I use PiCapes on older Minecraft versions?
Yes! PiCapes supports all Minecraft versions, including older ones. You can use the [ArcPi Proxy method](/other-guides/arcpi-proxy.md) to display your capes in clients that support OptiFine capes, which includes many older versions.

## Who can see my cape?
All other players that has either the PiCapes Fabric mod (Cape Provider X) or ArcPi Proxy installed can see your cape.
