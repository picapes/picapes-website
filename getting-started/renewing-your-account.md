# Renewing Your Account
Renewal gives you the **permission to change your cape**, renewal lasts 48hrs if you want to change your cape after those 48hrs you have to renew again.

## How to Renew ?
Open [PiCapes Discord Server](https://dsc.gg/picapes).
1. Open the `#⚡・machine` channel.
2. Run the command:

```text
/renew
```
3. Open the link.
![renew_eg]()

**NOTE:** It might show you a different message than this.

4. Click on `Start Renew`. ![Start Renew]()
5. Watch the video first so it'll be easy to navigate through GP Link's ads after that Click on `Continue`. ![GP Link Continue]()
6. After doing all the steps, Click on `Finish Renewal`
7. A message of "Successfully Renewed" will come.
